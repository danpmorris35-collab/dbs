The customer journey

A session with a beginning, a middle, and a next step.

You should feel that you are joining a safe, structured experience — not simply booking a hypnosis appointment.

Online via Zoom Private room, reliable connection, and headphones if you have them.
Five steps

The same shape, every time.

Step 01 Explore

Visit the website, find the pathway that sounds familiar, and read the suitability information.

Step 02 Enquire or book

Send an enquiry or use the eventual online booking system. You receive the next steps, terms, and any required questionnaire.

Step 03 Prepare

Choose a private space, review the consent information, and arrive without needing a perfect explanation.

Step 04 Attend

The session starts and ends on time, with a clear opening, therapeutic core, and practical close.

Step 05 Review

You receive appropriate aftercare and a recommendation about whether rebooking or another pathway is suitable.

Professional boundaries

Clear information is part of the care.

Before your first session, you will receive clear information about privacy, consent, preparation, cancellations, safeguarding, and what to do if you need more specialist support. Questions are always welcome before you decide whether to book.
