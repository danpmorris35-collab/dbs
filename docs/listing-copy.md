# Listing Copy — Google Business Profile, Yell, and everything else

Everything below is written to be pasted, not adapted. Character counts are
given where the platform enforces a limit, and every version is already inside
it.

**Paste, do not retype.** The whole point of section 0 in the discoverability

# Part 5 — The rules that get listings pulled

Short list, all of them things people do by accident.

- **Do not put keywords in the business name.** `North Point Creative — Web Design Liverpool` is a suspension risk, and competitors do report it. The name field is for the name
- **Do not put prices, offers or URLs in the Google description.** They belong in Products and the website field
- **Do not invent an address**, and do not use a virtual office you never sit in
- **Do not review yourself, buy reviews, or offer anything in exchange for one.** Do not ask only the clients you expect to be positive, either — review gating breaches the terms
- **Do not let the NAP drift.** One name, one number format, one address, everywhere
- **Do not buy bulk directory submissions.** Hundreds of listings with slightly-wrong details is precisely the inconsistency that damages you
