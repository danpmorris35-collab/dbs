Whichever door you open

The hour has the same shape every time.

First 15 Understand

We start with the pattern itself — what it does, when it shows up, what it costs you. There’s no history to dig through in front of anyone, and nothing you have to justify.

Middle 30 Reset

The hypnotherapy: a guided, deeply relaxed state where we work with the thoughts and responses sitting underneath the habit. You stay aware, in control, and able to stop at any point.

Last 15 Move forward

We come fully back to alertness, settle, and finish with one practical thing to take into the week. You’ll always know what your next step could be, without any pressure to take it.

Who holds the hour

Sarah.

I’m a hypnotherapist working exclusively with women, online. I started Positive Pathways because the women I met kept describing the same thing in slightly different words — capable, busy, holding everything together, and quietly running on a pattern they had never chosen.

My work is calm, practical and completely without judgement. You’ll never be sold something you don’t need, and you’ll always know exactly what the hour involves before it begins.
