About Positive Pathways

Support for the woman behind the pattern.

Positive Pathways is a women-focused hypnotherapy practice built around warmth, clarity, and a clear professional boundary around what this service can and cannot provide.

Sarah Hypnotherapist · online across the UK via Zoom
The approach

Warm, structured, non-judgemental.

The experience is designed so every client knows what happens next: suitability, intention, therapeutic work, practical close, and an honest next-step recommendation.

The boundary

Support, not a promise.

Hypnotherapy does not replace emergency care, medical diagnosis, prescribed treatment, or specialist mental-health care. If something sits outside competence, the right next step is referral.

Who holds the hour

Real women. Real change. Together.

I’m a hypnotherapist working exclusively with women, online. I started Positive Pathways because the women I met kept describing the same thing in slightly different words — capable, busy, holding everything together, and quietly running on a pattern they had never chosen.

My work is calm, practical and completely without judgement. You’ll never be sold something you don’t need, and you’ll always know exactly what the hour involves before it begins. Sarah brings a calm, practical approach to each session, with clear suitability boundaries and a focus on helping women take their next positive step with care.

  • Joining links are sent privately, never posted publicly.
  • Suitability is checked before your first booking.
  • Waiting room on. Every group is women only.
  • Written terms and consent before anything begins.
  • Sessions are never recorded.
  • Qualified and insured, with a referral route if needed.
