Before you book

The questions almost everyone asks.

It is sensible to want to know what hypnosis, Zoom, confidentiality, and group participation actually involve.

Clarity first No guaranteed outcomes. No pressure to book before suitability is discussed.
Will I be unconscious or lose control?

No. Hypnosis is a focused, relaxed state. You remain aware and able to choose what you do or do not engage with.

Does hypnotherapy work over Zoom?

Online delivery can be suitable for many people. Suitability is discussed before booking; you will need a private room and a reliable connection.

Will I have to talk in front of other women?

Not unless you want to. Group sessions are designed so you can take part without sharing personal details aloud.

How many sessions will I need?

That depends on your intention, circumstances, and suitability. A single session or three-session pathway may be appropriate, and the recommendation should be discussed rather than promised.

Is it confidential?

Private sessions are confidential within the practitioner’s professional and legal obligations. Group confidentiality is discussed clearly, but confidentiality between participants cannot be guaranteed in the same way.

Is this medical or specialist mental-health care?

No. Positive Pathways does not replace emergency care, medical diagnosis, prescribed treatment, or specialist mental-health support.
