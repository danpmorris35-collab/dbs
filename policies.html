Terms, privacy & cancellations.

A straightforward guide to how enquiries, sessions, privacy, and changes to appointments are handled.

Questions are welcome If something is unclear before you enquire, email hello@positivepathways.co.uk.
01 · Privacy — your information
Information shared through an enquiry is used to respond to your request, discuss suitability, and arrange a session when appropriate. It is not sold or used for unrelated marketing.

Please avoid sending urgent, highly sensitive, or emergency information through the enquiry form. If you need immediate help, contact emergency or crisis services in your area.
02 · Cancellations
If you need to change or cancel a session, please contact Sarah as soon as possible using the details provided in your confirmation. The practical notice period and any applicable fee will be explained before payment or booking.
03 · Suitability — what sessions are
Positive Pathways offers supportive hypnotherapy sessions for women online. Sessions are not emergency care, medical diagnosis, prescribed treatment, or a replacement for specialist mental-health support.

Suitability is discussed before booking. If your needs sit outside the practitioner’s competence, referral to more appropriate support is the responsible next step.
04 · Need to ask first?
You can ask about a session, delivery format, availability, or suitability before deciding whether to book. Send an enquiry →
